Enterprise IT Infrastructure & Support: Why Your 10-Year-Old Servers Are Costing You More Than You Think

By Marz Technology | 4 September 2026

The Hidden Cost of Legacy Infrastructure

If your business is still running on servers that are 5-10 years old, you're not just dealing with slow performance — you're bleeding money. Here's what most enterprises don't realise:

1. Energy Costs Have Skyrocketed

Older servers consume 3-5x more power than modern equivalents. With Malaysia's electricity tariffs increasing, a single old server can cost RM 3,000-5,000 more per year in energy alone compared to a modern replacement.

2. Downtime Risk Multiplies

Hardware older than 5 years has a 3x higher failure rate. Every hour of downtime costs enterprises an average of RM 25,000-100,000 depending on the industry. One major failure can wipe out months of savings from "not upgrading."

3. Security Vulnerabilities

Legacy systems often can't run the latest security patches. This leaves your business exposed to cyber threats. With cybersecurity incidents surging 40% in Malaysia's financial sector, running unpatched systems is a liability.

4. Staff Productivity Loss

Slow servers mean slow work. If 50 employees each lose 30 minutes daily waiting on slow systems, that's 25 hours of wasted productivity every single day — or roughly RM 15,000/month in lost productivity.

The Modern Enterprise IT Stack

Today's enterprise IT infrastructure should include:

  • Virtualisation: Run multiple servers on modern hardware, reducing physical footprint
  • Cloud Hybrid: Keep sensitive data on-premise while leveraging cloud for scalability
  • Managed Backup: Automated, tested backup and disaster recovery
  • 24/7 Monitoring: Proactive monitoring to catch issues before they become outages
  • Security Stack: Endpoint protection, firewall, email security, and employee training

ROI of Upgrading

Most enterprises that modernise their infrastructure see:

  • 40-60% reduction in energy costs
  • 99.9%+ uptime (vs. 95-97% with aging hardware)
  • 30% improvement in employee productivity
  • Significant reduction in security incidents
